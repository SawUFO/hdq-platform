Institutional Solutions

Your brand. Our intelligence.

HDQ produces daily financial intelligence for Canada’s wealth industry. Institutions can license it for their own publications and client communications, with HDQ attribution or fully under their own brand.

What HDQ produces

Five editorial desks (Market, Geopolitical, Economy, Tax & Wealth and Behavioural), the Daily Thread, the Weekend Edition and Month at a Glance, with original charts and named sources, in English and Canadian French. The content set covered by any agreement is defined individually.

The Daily Briefing

Five desks, every weekday morning: Market, Geopolitical, Economy, Tax & Wealth and Behavioural.

Daily Thread

The day’s most important development, examined across all five desks against closing data.

Weekend Edition and Month at a Glance

The week’s themes and the month’s data, with what they mean for portfolios.

Original charts and sourcing

Charts built from named sources, each with its source line and release date.

Two ways to publish

With HDQ attribution

HDQ is identified as the content provider alongside the institution’s own presentation.

  • Co-branded presentation of licensed HDQ content
  • Attribution wording and placement set by the agreement
  • Suited to institutions that want HDQ’s name to support the material

Fully white label

HDQ intelligence is distributed under the institution’s own brand.

  • Presented under the institution’s masthead and identity
  • Distribution under your brand does not make the material your own authored research; how it is described to readers is set by the agreement
  • Any credit required by law or by third-party sources is still respected

In both arrangements the institution is responsible for its own regulatory compliance and its communications with clients.

Possible uses

Subject to scoping and agreement:

  • Client commentary and market updates
  • Advisor-facing briefings
  • Firm websites and client portals
  • Branded PDF or email editions
  • Internal research distribution

Delivery formats and implementation arrangements are determined individually through institutional agreements.

Editorial methodology and independence

HDQ is produced under human editorial direction using a proprietary AI-assisted editorial framework, with named sources, source lines on every chart and published corrections. HDQ carries no advertising, accepts no sponsored content and takes no payment for coverage. A licence governs distribution and branding. It does not give an institution control over HDQ’s analysis or conclusions. Adaptations are possible only as separately agreed, and the institution is responsible for any it makes.

Rights and boundaries

Licensed rights are defined by agreement: content set, languages, term, territory, channels, attribution, and permitted modifications.

  • Photographs, market data and other third-party material are not assumed to be included. Where cleared for a particular licensee, they are specified in the agreement.
  • Public availability is not a licence: HDQ content is not public domain or openly licensed.
  • HDQ content is educational. It is not investment, tax or legal advice and is not compliance-approved.

Who may license

Financial advisory firms, wealth management organizations, dealers and other institutions serving Canadian investors.

How it works

Three steps from enquiry to your first branded edition.

01
You submit the intake document
A one-page form covering your firm name, brand colours, logo, preferred delivery time, and the client segments you want to reach. Takes about 15 minutes.
02
We build your branded template
HDQ applies your firm's brand to the publication template. You review and approve. Typical turnaround is three business days from intake submission.
03
Your edition arrives every morning
From go-live, your clients receive a branded HDQ edition each weekday by 7 a.m. Eastern. You do nothing. We write, edit, and deliver every issue.

What your firm receives

Five-desk daily briefing

Eligibility & Verification

HDQ is published exclusively for CIRO-registered investment advisors and representatives and Certified Financial Planners (CFP) practising in Canada. We do not accept applications from retail investors, students, or unlicensed individuals regardless of their financial background or interest.

Every application is manually reviewed. We verify each applicant's registration status against the CIRO AdvisorReport and the CSA National Registration Search before approving access. Applications that cannot be verified will not be approved, and the application fee will not be charged.

HDQ reserves the right to decline any application without explanation and to revoke access if a subscriber's registration status changes or if the publication's terms of use are violated.

Questions about eligibility: support@hdq.ca
